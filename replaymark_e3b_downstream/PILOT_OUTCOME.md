## What the pilot establishes for protocol design

The implementation is suitable for a confirmatory boundary experiment. The primary engineering decision can be stated without a fitted SLA or post-hoc threshold:

> What is the minimum real Mosquitto `max_queued_messages` setting that preserves all QoS 1 messages generated by one 128-task target batch for an offline persistent consumer?

The frozen E3b semantic counts independently predict two exact boundaries: `512` under R1 and `768` under target-native semantics. The confirmatory experiment will test the one-message neighborhoods of those two boundaries on independent runners rather than reusing the broad exploratory sweep.
